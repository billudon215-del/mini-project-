Describe the work.
The model finds the freelancer.

Freelancer Web Place ranks every profile against your actual requirement text — not just keyword filters — and re-ranks by real track record: rating, reviews, and completed projects.

How the matching works

Three modules, one ranked shortlist.

01 · TEXT
Requirement → Vector

Your free-text brief is vectorized with TF-IDF and compared to every freelancer profile using cosine similarity.

02 · TRACK RECORD
Quality re-ranking

A Random Forest model scores reliability from experience, rating, reviews, and completed projects.

03 · SHORTLIST
Top 10, blended

Scores are blended and returned as a ranked shortlist — each card shows its match ring.
